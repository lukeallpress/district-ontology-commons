# Question ledger: what people and agents could not answer about how we operate

Every "how do we..." that went unanswered goes here, with who asked (a role or an agent), when, and how often
it has come up. A question closes when a record answers it, and the closing line names the record. An agent
that cannot answer a question from the records **adds a line here instead of guessing**; that is the mechanism
by which the ontology learns what it is missing.

Rules: one line per question; `asked_by` is a role or `agent`; `count` goes up when the same question returns;
row numbers are used once and never reused; a row that names infrastructure, or starts with `[internal]` or
`[restricted]`, is not shown below that level.
